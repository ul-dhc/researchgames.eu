Livonian Place Names

Match place names in Livonian and Latvian and explore the place-name heritage of the Livonians, Latvia’s Indigenous people.

  • Based on the Catalogue of Livonian Place Names.
  • Made with the Livonian Institute of the University of Latvia.

Latvian Riddle Grid

Solve Latvian folk riddles, find their answers in a letter grid and optionally help researchers enrich the folklore dataset.

  • Based on Latvian folklore records from Garamantas.lv.
  • Made with the Institute of Literature, Folklore and Art, University of Latvia.
