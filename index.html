About

Research you can play

ResearchGames.eu is a new, experimental and internationally oriented initiative with roots in the University of Latvia Digital Humanities Center, exploring how publications, datasets and discoveries can become playable digital experiences.

At its core is a simple idea: what happens when research is not only presented through papers and datasets, but made playable?

We create browser-based games inspired by real publications, datasets and research practices. Each game connects back to the research behind it, giving players another way to encounter scientific and scholarly work.

Alongside individual games, we are developing reusable formats that can allow similar game mechanics to be adapted to other research and datasets.
